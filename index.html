About the groups

Connect — bring ideas together or carry an idea across.
Edit — add, remove, replace or separate parts.
Reshape — change order, scale, direction or repetition.

These are navigation groups. Boden’s three forms describe a creative process: combining ideas, exploring within a set of rules, or changing those rules. A move here can serve more than one form, depending on how you use it.
